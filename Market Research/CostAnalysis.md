## Google Maps Platform Costs (Based on Indian Pricing)
For this project, the following Google Maps Platform services will be required:

### 1. Geocoding (India)  
**Purpose:** To convert employee addresses to coordinates  
-  70,000 free calls per month  
-  Beyond 70,000: **$1.50 per 1,000 calls** (up to 5 million)  

### 2. Routes: Compute Routes Essentials (India)  
**Purpose:** For basic routing  
-  70,000 free calls per month  
-  Beyond 70,000: **$1.50 per 1,000 calls** (up to 5 million)  

### 3. Dynamic Maps (India)  
**Purpose:** For visualization  
-  70,000 free calls per month  
-  Beyond 70,000: **$2.10 per 1,000 calls** (up to 5 million)  

### 4. Route Optimization - Fleet Routing (India)  
**Purpose:** For multi-vehicle optimization  
-  7,000 free calls per month  
-  Beyond 7,000: **$2.40 per 1,000 calls** (up to 5 million)
